PEOPLE / Tokyo

Ichiro Erokumae

Editor / Head of Food HEROes

Telling the stories of food, nurturing new culture.

Former deputy editor of the food magazine Cuisine Kingdom (Ryori Okoku), he became independent in 2020. He has covered more than 400 restaurant visits over the course of his career.

He sees restaurants as a medium where people meet and encounter new perspectives. For him, the role of media is to create new culture. With this idea in mind, he founded Food HEROes to support the next generation of chefs.

At SHIZEN WO SHOKUSU, he documents the words exchanged around the table and the lives of the people and places behind them, in words and photographs.

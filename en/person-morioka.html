PEOPLE / Shiga · Shigaraki

Kasho Morioka

Ceramic artist / Fourth-generation Kashogama

The fourth-generation ceramic artist of Kashogama, founded in Gojozaka, Kyoto, in 1914. With its main premises in Kyoto and a workshop in Shigaraki, Shiga, he pursues expression through clay, from tea utensils to vessels for the table.

In June 2026, he was featured as a speaker at the international gastronomy congress Madrid Fusión Alimentos de España Atelier Tokyo 2026, on the theme of “Beauty as Gastronomy.”

Through ENSOU PROJECT, he works with KIFUTATO WINES in Hara Village, Nagano, exploring new approaches to winemaking using ceramic vessels.

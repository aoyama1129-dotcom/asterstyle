Kasho Kiln / Kashogama

Kyoto · Shigaraki / Established 1914

Kashogama is a Kyoto ceramics workshop established in Gojozaka in 1914. Its tradition of tea ceramics has continued through four generations. Today the company is based in Kiyomizu, Kyoto, with a workshop in Shigaraki, Shiga.

Four generations of ceramics

The second generation trained with the Raku family. The third exhibited tea, flower and dining vessels; the fourth-generation artist, Kasho Morioka (森岡嘉祥), works with both traditional techniques and contemporary uses of ceramics.

From vessels for chefs to vessels for wine

From tea utensils and dining vessels to ceramic vessels for wine, Kashogama has explored new uses for clay. The ENSOU PROJECT brings the kiln’s craftsmanship in Shigaraki together with the winemaking of KIFUTATO WINES in Hara Village.

ENSOU PROJECT

In 2026, Morioka and winemaker Kiriko Hitachi developed an egg-shaped amphora for orange wine. The finished vessel was delivered to Hara Village in October. Our story records the development from initial discussions to production and delivery.
